# Last Cue assets and references

The fictional theatre takes its machinery and spatial vocabulary from
[Alexandra Palace's preserved understage traps, pulleys and lifts](https://www.alexandrapalace.com/blog/scanning-stage-machinery/)
and the [V&A's account of moving scenery and theatrical transformations](https://www.vam.ac.uk/articles/the-story-of-pantomime).
These are research references; no photographs, scans, institution logos or
third-party game assets are distributed with the project.

`scripts/last-cue-art.py` generates the original modular theatre kit, an
articulated stagehand based on our original traveler rig, and six original PCM
WAV sounds. Geometry, rig, animation clips, audio and generator code use the
repository's MIT license. `studio.hdr` reuses our original procedural studio
environment from Material Gallery.

`assets/wood.png` and `assets/velvet.png` were generated with the built-in
imagegen tool using the imagegen skill.
They were copied unchanged into the project and visually inspected. Geometry
supplies the curtain folds. Original generation prompts follow.

## Stage boards

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: seamless square diffuse albedo texture for original low-poly 3D backstage theatre adventure Last Cue. Orthographic straight-on material plane filling entire 1024x1024 image. Worn theatre stage flooring: long dark warm honey-brown oak boards, staggered end joints, old nail heads, subtle scuffs and scratched paint traces, restrained hand-painted texture with broad readable shapes and realistic wood grain. Inspired by historical timber theatre machinery, rather than fantasy ruins. Neutral flat illumination, no directional shadows, no perspective, no objects, no text, no borders. Opposite edges must tile seamlessly. Use moderate contrast so player silhouettes and brass machinery read clearly.

## Curtain velvet

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: seamless square diffuse albedo texture for velvet stage curtains in an original 3D theatre game Last Cue. A uniform straight-on 1024x1024 material swatch of worn deep oxblood crimson theatrical velvet, subtle soft woven pile and broad irregular faded patches. Texture only: do not draw large curtain folds because the 3D mesh supplies folds. Flat neutral illumination, no directional shadows or gloss, no props, no lettering, no border, no perspective. Opposite edges tile seamlessly. Rich muted red palette with restrained variation, readable in warm stage lighting.
